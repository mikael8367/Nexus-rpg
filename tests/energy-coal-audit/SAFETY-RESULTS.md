## Changes in this worktree
* `TileCoalGenerator`: kept the existing unknown-root, incompatible numeric and fuel-archive preservation changes. Invalid numeric energy/timer now become zero, with original NBT held opaque. Structurally malformed inventory is archived as a whole and never activated; valid legacy items remain extractable. Any `NexusRpgSchemaVersion` marker is treated as unsupported: energy, burn and inventory are archived, operation and normal insertion disabled. This is a conservative convention, not a general version detection mechanism.
* `BlockPowerMachine`: refuse player removal and explosion callback for coal tiles with preserved archives, unknown roots, or serialized ForgeData/ForgeCaps. `getDrops` also suppresses drops for such tiles, since `Explosion.doExplosionB` drops before calling `onBlockExploded`, otherwise it can duplicate the protected block. Snapshot restoration skips coal inventory extraction in `breakBlock`.
* Added `CoalGuardTest.java` and `run-safety.sh`. Existing break test and other pre-existing tests remain unchanged.

## Outstanding risks / next steps
* No opaque-data item transport or placement/reinstallation exists. Never claim safe movement or universal preservation. Design a single non-stackable recovery carrier with safe validation, collision-safe opaque structure, explicit placement semantics and no reactivation, coordinate/id rewriting, and careful item-event lifecycle. Prove no duplicate machine/inventory in creative, drops disabled, explosion, cancellation, snapshots, silk touch, automation and mod interactions. Do not rehydrate archives as live items.
* Direct/world/setblock/mod removals still lose archives and unknown data. The mitigation only gates two callbacks and is not a general world-level deletion prevention mechanism. Snapshot rollback behavior beyond extraction is not proven.
* The unknown-version rule recognizes only the specific `NexusRpgSchemaVersion` key. Future formats without it may still be interpreted by this version. No arbitrary future schema safety guarantee. Save/read of `ForgeData`, `ForgeCaps`, coordinates and id uses Forge superclass behavior; malformed/unknown data under those reserved names is not guaranteed preserved.
* Normal drops, actual creative/survival, explosion effects, event ordering/cancellation, modded removal, full snapshot rollback, placement, chunk and disk serialization, and behavior with genuinely registered item/block all require integrated Forge tests. A normal block without archives still follows old destruction behavior. Protected blocks cannot be player-mined or destroyed by the guarded explosion callback until migration is implemented.
* Test artifacts and `logs/` were produced and intentionally retained under worktree; disk ~117 MiB free at test completion. Avoid deleting user's data/caches. The detached HEAD was not synchronized with the remote; integration must be reviewed on the intended branch without overwriting existing work.
