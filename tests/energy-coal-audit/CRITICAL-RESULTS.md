## Changes and test expectations
* A tile with any `NexusRpgSchemaVersion` remains disabled; Energy, BurnTicks, FuelInventory and the marker retain their original NBT types and values at their original keys, including missing keys, on repeated saves. A conflicting caller-provided output key fails closed. Insertion, direct nonempty slot assignment and inventory deserialization are refused. This only detects the named marker; an unmarked unknown format is not guaranteed safe.
* Inventories whose complete serialization differs from their source (including foreign metadata), or are malformed, are stored only as opaque archives; their items are not additionally activated. `CoalNbtAuditTest`'s old expectation explicitly required the same fuel to be live and archived, and was changed for anti-duplication. Its undiverged coal/charcoal case still checks 12 live items and no archive. `CoalGuardTest`'s outdated future-inventory archive expectation was adjusted: the future inventory is passive at the original key and is not duplicated as an archive.
* ForgeData compounds continue as active Forge data. Unknown ForgeCaps keys are merged back under their original names, while attached provider serializers win for their own keys, so live changes are retained. Wrong-type ForgeData/ForgeCaps are retained raw if no active data needs the same key. If malformed raw roots and nonempty active Forge data need that key simultaneously, saving throws `IllegalStateException` instead of silently overwriting or duplicating either. Conflicting caller-provided Forge output values also throw. This is a deliberate hard stop, not transparent successful saving: real-world chunk saving/crash/recovery behavior has not been proven. Do not merge, distribute or deploy this work.
* Forge dispatches registered provider deserialization by name and can throw if a provider rejects an unexpected per-capability NBT type; this path has not been validated in a running Forge environment. Unknown capability merging assumes Forge's registered serializable writers accurately represent the keys they own.

## Critical remaining blockers
The previous recovery carrier is not a general conservation solution. Actual snapshot capture/commit/rollback remains untested; the existing mock checks suppression, not correct commit/rollback. Forced break during capture can erase contents, and forced break with doTileDrops=false can erase contents. Spawning/cancellation, carrier destruction, crashes, direct replacements by other mods and event ordering remain risks. Recovery carrier is inert and no recovery UI/automatic import exists. The passing direct-break test checks only its simulated case and does not prove forced removal or snapshots fixed.

The fail-closed Forge serialization exceptions can interrupt saving and possibly impact a live world; integrated testing and a safe operational migration/recovery design are required before any merge, distribution or deployment. Disk was ~115 MiB free; existing other-agent edits and generated outputs were retained. No claim of global bug-freedom.
